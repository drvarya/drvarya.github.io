Disclaimer

This is scenario modeling, not financial, tax, or lending advice. Figures are estimates for comparing payoff paths — they are not a quote, commitment, or recommendation. You are responsible for verifying all numbers, terms, and assumptions with your lender or servicer before making any decision. Illustrative fixed-rate amortization only — no taxes, insurance, PMI, HOA, or points. Payments and interest are rounded to the nearest cent; the last payment clears any leftover balance so principal totals match the loan. Biweekly is 26 half-payments a year applied immediately (true biweekly), not a servicer that holds drafts until a full monthly payment posts. A recast is a lender service, not a refinance: the rate and remaining term stay the same, the payment is rebuilt on the new balance. Confirm terms, recast eligibility, biweekly drafting, and fees with your servicer. Monthly P&I is calculated on the loan amount, not the purchase price.
